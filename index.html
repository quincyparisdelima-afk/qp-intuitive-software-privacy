Privacy Policy

App: Reminder by QP
Developer: QP Intuitive Software
Effective date: October 15, 2026

QP Intuitive Software respects your privacy. This policy explains how Reminder by QP handles information when you use the Android application.

Information you enter

Reminder by QP lets you create and manage reminders, including titles, notes, scheduled dates and times, and reminder status. This information is stored in a database on your device for the app to function.

Data collection and sharing

The app is designed to work offline. It does not require an account, and it does not transmit reminder content to servers operated by QP Intuitive Software. The app does not incorporate advertising or analytics SDKs. QP Intuitive Software does not sell or share reminder content with advertisers.

Device permissions

The app may request notification permission to display reminder alerts and exact alarm access to deliver reminders at scheduled times. It also receives device restart notifications so that reminders can be scheduled again after a reboot. These capabilities are used for reminder functionality, not tracking.

Device backup

Android's system-managed backup or device-transfer features may include eligible app data, depending on your device, operating system, and backup settings. Such processing is controlled by your Android device and backup provider, not by QP Intuitive Software. You can review your device's backup settings to manage these features.

Data retention and deletion

Your reminder information remains in the app's local storage until you delete it using available app controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Depending on your Android backup settings, a separate system-managed backup copy may persist according to your backup provider's policies.

Children's privacy

Reminder by QP is a general-purpose reminder utility. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated services.

Security

Reminder information is stored in Android app-specific local storage. Device-level security, backups, and access to the phone can affect the protection of locally stored information.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or its privacy practices change. We will publish the revised policy on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact QP Intuitive Software at qpintuitivesoftware@gmail.com.
